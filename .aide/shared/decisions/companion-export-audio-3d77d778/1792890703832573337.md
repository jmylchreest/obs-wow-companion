# companion-export-audio

**Decision:** Precise exports use PCM audio in temporary parts and AAC encoding once after concatenation, with explicit part durations.

## Rationale

A 20-segment regression demonstrated cumulative AAC encoder priming caused export duration failure. The corrected path preserves 600 evenly spaced video frames and continuous audio on two tracks; temporary audio disk cost is documented.
