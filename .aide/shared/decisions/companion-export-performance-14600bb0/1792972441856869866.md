# companion-export-performance

**Decision:** Default automatic exports to Fast copy with a one-time profile migration; preserve subsequent explicit choices and stored job modes. Keep CPU/NVENC/QSV/AMF/VAAPI choices for precise exports. Concatenate compatible continuous fragments after comparing codec parameters, time bases and extradata; persist keyframe-adjusted origins. Retain full final validation and CPU fallback with actual encoder metadata. Explicit range exports remain precise. FFmpeg and ffprobe remain external.
