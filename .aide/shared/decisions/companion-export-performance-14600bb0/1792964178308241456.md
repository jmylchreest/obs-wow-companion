# companion-export-performance

**Decision:** Keep precise CPU exports as the backward-compatible default. Advanced settings allow automatic Fast copy exports and explicit CPU/NVENC/QSV/AMF/VAAPI encoding for precise cuts. Directly concatenate compatible continuous fragments after comparing codec parameters, time bases and extradata; align fast starts to keyframes and persist the real origin. Retain full output validation. Retry failed copying or GPU exports as a whole on CPU, recording actual encoder and fallback in sidecar metadata and Video details. Manual range cuts remain precise. Media executables remain independently configurable and external.
