# companion-playback-compatibility

**Decision:** Attempt original recording playback first with the embedded player. On a playback error, generate a cached MP4 for the selected audio track; stream-copy H.264 yuv420p video and transcode other video formats. Preserve originals, coalesce repeated selections, and serialize preview work. Hide generic recording-end signposts but retain boss-end events.

## Rationale

Avoid unnecessary full-video encoding and duplicate-request errors while retaining a compatibility path for different platform decoders.
