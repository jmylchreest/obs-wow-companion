# companion-death-recaps

**Decision:** Store up to three observed incoming player damage events within ten seconds on each death marker, with death-relative offsets, source and ability. Bound and checkpoint buffers per installation; clear on death, resurrection, source change or zone change. Label as recent hits, not a guaranteed killing blow. Preserve source and playback position across Settings navigation.
