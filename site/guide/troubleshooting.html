PLAYER GUIDE

Troubleshooting

Use the status pills and Events to find what is waiting or blocked.

OBS will not connect

OBS offline means the configured WebSocket endpoint is unreachable. Check that OBS is open, its server is enabled and the port matches. It does not distinguish a closed OBS process from a disabled server. Companion retries automatically, with a delay up to 30 seconds; click the pill for details.

Password store means the system credential store could not be used. Unlock it or enter the OBS password in setup for this session. An authentication error means OBS answered but did not accept the credentials. These are separate from offline errors.

An encounter did not record

Check Auto recording is on, setup is complete, the selected Logs folder belongs to the running game, and /combatlog is enabled. Check the installation's minimum difficulty/key policy. Delves are off by default; Classic and PvP coverage still needs wider real-client testing.

Starting Companion mid-dungeon may miss the start event. Record now captures from that point and can still collect later markers. It cannot recover earlier footage. The default inactivity timeout is five minutes; a detected departure from a Mythic+ instance shortens abandonment to 60 seconds. Missing or delayed log events can affect detection. Stop recording lets you end a stuck activity early.

A finished fight is not ready

The app may be waiting for extra footage after the encounter, OBS file closure, another media job, encoding or validation. Events shows periodic FFmpeg progress. Manual file splitting in OBS helps make closed footage available.

If OBS was already recording when Companion connected, that recording remains yours. Stop it in OBS when ready; Companion will not stop or split it. Original video is played where supported; other containers, codecs or audio selections may require a compatible copy.

Recover missing footage

Open Settings → Advanced → Check recordings. Stop recording in OBS first. If OBS is disconnected, confirm that it has stopped. The recovery panel reports progress and files needing attention.

Unrecoverable means usable matching footage was not found in the checked folder, or required parts are missing. Encounter details remain. Restore any original files to the temporary recording folder and check again. Uncertain timing and retryable export failures remain attention items; they are not proof that footage is lost.

Unrecognised review files are kept rather than silently deleted. Empty unrecoverable files offer a confirmed Delete file action. Recovery cannot reconstruct footage that was never recorded or safely guess an uncertain timeline.

Storage and quitting

Temporary recordings and saved clips have separate budgets, initially 30 GiB and 100 GiB, with a 2 GiB free-space threshold. Only eligible managed files are cleaned up. Protected recordings, active footage and unfinished jobs can exceed these working limits. A heart does not provide protection; use the lock.

Quit or closing the window stops capture Companion owns and preserves unfinished automatic clip plans for next launch. You do not need to pause first or wait for exports. Recordings started independently in OBS continue. Sharing ZIP exports need retrying after interruption.

If a job fails, fix free space, permissions or media-tool configuration, then retry. Cancel preparation preserves the sources for a later retry. Keep source footage until the saved clip is ready. A disk-full recording may be empty or incomplete.

Send a useful report

Open Events with the arrow at the bottom right. Drag its top edge to resize it; use Filter, Follow or Save log as needed. Include the Companion and OBS versions, OS/desktop, WoW version and what you were doing in a test report or bug report. Remove passwords, private session links, personal paths and player names before attaching logs or screenshots.

OBS is still recording after a restart

After a crash, Companion reads missed combat-log events from its saved position before deciding whether the key has ended. A continuing key keeps its original entry and markers. A logged completion finishes it; leaving the instance uses the 60-second grace period, and missing activity uses your inactivity timeout (five minutes by default). Restarting alone does not abandon a key.

Companion also checks the saved OBS endpoint, output file, recording duration and byte counters against the running output. If these confirm the same recording, it keeps capturing while the key is active and stops after post-roll once no activity needs it. The same scene alone is not proof of ownership. Separately started or unverified recordings remain under your control. Normal Quit still ends Companion-owned capture.

Clear the events log

Use Clear log beside Collapse in Events to remove the saved diagnostic history. New events continue to appear. Recordings, markers and combat logs are unaffected. Use Save log first if you want to keep a copy.
