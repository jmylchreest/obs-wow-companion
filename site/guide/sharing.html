PLAYER GUIDE

Share a replay

Export a video with its markers and a small web player.

Create a player bundle

  1. Select a ready recording and expand Export. It is collapsed initially.
  2. Keep the whole video, or choose Select range and drag the timeline handles. You can also enter start/end times.
  3. Select the audio tracks to include: one track, several mixed into one, or none for a silent video.
  4. Choose a new .zip filename and click Export ZIP. The folder button opens a save dialog in the desktop app; browser-only mode accepts a path directly.

Track 1 may already contain the complete OBS mix. Including it alongside the separate game or microphone tracks can duplicate sound. Mixing normalises the selected inputs; it is not a loudness mastering pass.

Existing files are not overwritten. Export progress appears in the panel and Events. You can cancel an export; wait for cancellation to finish before retrying. Closing Companion cancels sharing work, which needs an explicit retry next time. Automatic clip preparation is separate and can resume after restart.

Compatible whole-video exports copy H.264/AAC media without re-encoding. Precise cuts and incompatible media need conversion. The result is checked before the ZIP is published, so “Checking saved video and audio” can take time after encoding has finished.

Watch or host it

Send the ZIP to your group. Extract it and open index.html. If local video access is blocked, use Open video to choose the included video.mp4. Keep the whole folder together.

The bundle includes the video, a poster, recording metadata, player assets and licence notices. The player works without Companion or an account. It does not contact external services. You can upload the extracted folder to a static host with MP4 byte-range support; uploading the ZIP alone does not create a website.

Check before sharing

The bundle omits local paths, source plans and installation identifiers. It still contains visible chat, player names, audio, labels and marker text. Review the clip and selected audio before sending it. Companion does not automatically obscure these details or upload recordings.

Find previous exports

Video details shows the original video and the latest successful exported bundle, including its location, selected range and tracks. Failed exports do not replace the previous success. Moving a ZIP outside the app does not update that saved location.

Deleting a library recording does not delete independently exported ZIPs. Community accounts, uploads and library import from bundles are not implemented.
