PLAYER GUIDE

Review recordings

Find a run, jump to a death and see what happened.

Find and keep recordings

Search names or labels, then narrow the list by activity, outcome, difficulty, key level or date. Difficulty and key choices come from your library. The heart and lock filters show favourites and protected recordings. Sort by newest, oldest or name.

The date picker applies a start/end range. Escape, Cancel or clicking outside discards a draft; the clear button or All dates resets it. The recording list scrolls independently. Drag its divider to resize it, or collapse it for more player space.

Labels save automatically. A heart marks a favourite; a lock protects a recording from deletion and quota cleanup. Favourites alone do not protect footage. Delete asks for confirmation; Shift-click skips that confirmation, but protected recordings still need unlocking. Deleting a library entry removes its saved video and sidecar; independently exported ZIPs are kept.

Player and markers

Boss combat is red on the timeline; estimated combat outside boss fights is orange. Click a signpost to seek to its event. Death rows seek five seconds before the death and show up to three recent observed hits when available. These are not a guaranteed killing-blow analysis.

The eye control filters marker categories or toggles all of them. These preferences persist. The flag control adds a manual marker. Audio selection, playback speed and fullscreen are in the player. Switching audio tracks retains your position; first use may need a compatible playback copy.

Mouse-wheel zoom magnifies the picture; drag to pan. This changes the view, not the recording. Unavailable footage has no active playback controls.

Recording controls

Record now starts a manual clip. Stop recording can end a manual or automatic recording early while retaining its captured footage. The Auto recording pill pauses or resumes future automatic recording; it is separate from manual capture.

There is no live-stream player. Closed, continuous footage may become reviewable while final preparation is still running. The original is used where playable; otherwise Companion prepares a compatible copy and leaves the source intact.

Settings → Export chooses Fast copy or precise encoding for automatic clips. Fast copy avoids quality loss but can include extra footage at a keyframe. Selected sharing ranges are precise. GPU choices fall back to CPU when unavailable. Video details shows the source and the method actually used.
